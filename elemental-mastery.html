Three Orbs, Three Different Benefits

Activate one passive orb at a time: Fire boosts movement speed, Frost improves jumping, and Life regenerates health and mana.

How I built it

I built an orb manager that connects the selected orb to movement speed, jump strength, or health and mana regeneration. Separate follow and swirl behavior keeps the orbs moving around the player.

Enemies That Patrol & Pursue

Enemies patrol the world and chase the player, adding pressure to exploration and platforming.

How I built it

I built patrol and chase behavior with supporting animation and enemy health scripts. This connects enemy movement, visual reactions, and defeat behavior to the combat system.

Summoned Boss Encounter

Collect elemental cores to summon the final boss. Defeating it completes the game.

How I built it

I connected core collection to a summoning altar that brings the final boss into play, giving the player a goal that leads into the last encounter.

My Contribution

Solo game design and gameplay programming in Unity. I built the orb effects, enemy patrol and pursuit, and core-based boss summoning, then integrated their animation and visual feedback into a short playable prototype.
