Character Switching

Switch characters while the camera and HUD update to show who you control.

How I built it

PartySwitchManager routes input to the active character, swaps character cameras, and sends a change event. ProfileHudSwapper uses Tween to animate the active portrait and health display.

Connected Puzzle Progress

Collect heart pieces, combine them, and bring both characters together to activate the escape portal.

How I built it

A shared inventory stores pickups and notifies the UI when items change. HeartCombineOnContact checks for the required pieces. The portal checks for the combined heart and both characters in its trigger before activation.

Camera-Guided Interactions

Activate gates and reveal items, with camera focus and sound helping show what changed.

How I built it

Gate and item-spawn sequences coordinate timed actions with optional Cinemachine camera focus. During focus, movement input is paused; afterward, control returns to the active character.

My Contribution

I built the mechanics and code that turn the level’s puzzles and artwork into a playable experience.

  • Character controls: movement, jumping, interaction, and switching between the two characters.
  • Puzzle systems: gates, item spawning, heart-piece collection and combination, and portal activation.
  • UI implementation: shared inventory, drag-and-drop slots, interaction prompts, and animated character HUDs.
  • Hazards & recovery: timed spike traps, damage, health regeneration, and respawning both characters.
