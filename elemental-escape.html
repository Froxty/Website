Fire & Frost Puzzles

Switch between fire and frost to solve environmental puzzles. Burn objects with fire and freeze blocks with frost to change how you move through the level.

How I built it

I implemented the elemental interactions and ability switching that let players use different powers to overcome obstacles. Supporting interaction scripts handle moving blocks with timed resets and teleport points with on-screen prompts.

Spell Combat & Mana

Aim and cast projectiles while keeping track of your mana. Each shot uses mana, which begins regenerating after a delay.

How I built it

A ray from the center of the camera determines the aim point, and a physics projectile travels toward it from the casting position. The attack checks for enough mana before firing. Separate scripts manage mana regeneration, damage, and animated resource displays.

Fire Dragon Encounter

Face a dragon that summons swirl of flame-nados. Can you dodge his barrage attack and bring down the dragon.

How I built it

I coordinated dialogue, animation playback, and transform movement for the introduction and takeoff. Defined zones shape the attack pattern, while damage scripts attached to the tornado effects turn the visuals into hazards. Frost provides the elemental counter to the fire dragon.

My Contribution

I programmed the systems that connect puzzle interactions, spell combat, and the boss encounter into a playable experience.

  • Elemental gameplay: switching between fire and frost and using each ability to interact with puzzle objects.
  • Combat & resources: projectile aiming, damage handling, player health, mana costs, and delayed mana regeneration.
  • Boss implementation: dialogue sequencing, animated takeoff, transform movement, and tornado attacks within defined zones.
  • Enemy behavior: navigation-based pursuit, health tracking, and configurable collectible drops.
  • UI & interactions: animated health and mana bars, camera-facing enemy health displays, and teleport prompts.

Project Focus

Using the right element for the challenge.

Fire and frost give players different ways to solve obstacles. The dragon encounter carries that same idea into combat: recognizing its fire element tells the player to use frost.

My work focused on gameplay programming, system integration, and coordinating the interactions and feedback that make these mechanics function together.
