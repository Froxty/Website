About Me

I’m a generalist game developer with a strong programming foundation and a genuine love for every part of the game-development pipeline. I mainly work in Unity and Roblox (Lua), and I enjoy building gameplay systems, creating UI/UX, and working through the full 3D pipeline from modeling and texturing to animation and VFX. I love how many different arts come together in game development, and I’m always exploring new tools and workflows to make better experiences.

I’m a systems-minded developer who focuses on mechanics that feel responsive, readable, and fun. I iterate quickly, write clean and modular code, and use VFX, sound, and smart UI to give players strong feedback. I also understand secure client–server relationships and how to keep important logic server-side to prevent exploits and maintain fair gameplay.

Tools I use: Unity (URP/HDRP, Shader Graph, VFX Graph), C#, Roblox Studio (Lua), Blender, Substance 3D Painter, and Git.
